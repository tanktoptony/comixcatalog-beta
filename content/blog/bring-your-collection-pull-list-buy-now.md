If you already track your comics somewhere else, the last thing you want is to type 800 books in a second time. I get it. I wouldn't do it either. So that's the first thing on this list.

## Bring your collection over

The [Switch page](https://www.comixcatalog.com/switch) takes an export from CLZ Comics, from League of Comic Geeks, or from any spreadsheet. Each one comes with steps for getting the file out of the other app. League's Excel file goes straight in. No converting it to anything first.

Then it guesses which of your columns is which. You fix anything it guessed wrong, and it matches every row to the catalog:

- Grades, slab company, cert numbers, what you paid and your notes come along.
- When a title has more than one volume with that issue number, it asks which one you meant, and it shows whether each choice is the comic, the trade paperback or the hardcover when we know.
- Anything we can't find goes on a "Not in our catalog yet" list, with a button that sends me a request to add it.
- If the file is a wishlist, flip it to "On my wantlist" and the books land there instead of in your collection.

## A pull list

On any series page, hit **+ Add to pull list**. Your [pull list](https://www.comixcatalog.com/pull-list) then shows new issues from the series you follow, week by week, as they land in the catalog. **Got it** adds one to your collection. **Want** puts it on your wantlist. Below that is what's coming up, month by month.

## Barcode scanning, for everybody

The [Scan page](https://www.comixcatalog.com/scan) has a Barcode tab now, open to every member. Line up the UPC and the small 5-digit block next to it. Switch on Keep scanning to go through a stack. Barcode reads don't count against your 100 cover scans a day.

We don't have a barcode for every book yet. If one comes up empty, tap Snap the cover instead and it falls back to the cover scan.

## Buy now

The marketplace has a real checkout. Set up payouts once (Stripe asks for your ID and a bank account, and I never see either), and every book you list with a price and a condition or slab grade gets a Buy now button. Buyers pay by card.

There are no ComixCatalog fees during the beta. Stripe's card processing (2.9% + 30¢) comes out of your payout, and every order shows the breakdown. Shipping times, when you get paid and the limits for new sellers are all on the [Sell page](https://www.comixcatalog.com/sell). Listings without a price still take offers by message, same as before.

## Search that knows what an edition is

Search shows one entry per series now, with its trade paperbacks, hardcovers and other editions grouped under it instead of scattered down the page. You can filter results by format, decade and publisher. A comic with trades or hardcovers also gets a page that lists every edition of it in one place.

## A home page about your books

Sign in and the home page is Your shelf:

- your counts: books, keys, complete runs, wantlist
- what you added recently
- what's new this week from your pull list
- a few runs you're close to finishing, with the missing issue numbers and how many of them are for sale right now

## Smaller fixes

- **The insurance PDF shows your own photos the right way up.** Photos from a phone were coming out sideways in the PDF, even though they looked fine on the site. That's fixed, and the covers don't sit on top of the titles anymore either.
- **Select a range reaches the end of a long run.** On a 650-issue series you used to have to hit Show all first. Now it shows the whole run when you start a range.
- **Sign in shows up on phones.** It was hiding.
- **You can put ComixCatalog on your phone's home screen** and it opens like an app.

If you've been holding off because your books already live in another app, [start on the Switch page](https://www.comixcatalog.com/switch). The first 100 members are still Founding Collectors: a numbered badge nobody after No. 100 can get, your name on the founders wall, and a lifetime membership.
