I've had people ask me how to find their friends on here. The honest answer was "know their exact username and type it into the address bar like it's 1997." Not great.

So that got fixed, along with a pile of other things. Here's what's new.

## Follow people

There's a new [People page](https://www.comixcatalog.com/people). Search for collectors with public profiles by username or name, hit Follow, done. It's one way, like Instagram: no friend requests, no awkward waiting. Your profile shows how many followers you have and how many people you follow, the People page lists them, and the little inbox icon up top lights up when someone new follows you.

If you want to follow a bunch of people who actually collect, the top of the People page has a Collectors to follow list. Everybody on it is preselected. One tap follows the whole bunch.

Private profiles stay private. You can't find them in search, and if you set yours to private you can still see your own profile, with a note that only you can see it.

## Scan a whole stack

On the [Scan page](https://www.comixcatalog.com/scan) there's now a Scan a stack mode. Point your phone at a cover, snap, next cover, snap. You don't wait for each one to finish. When you're done, you get one list with every match, and right there in the list you can:

- swap a match if it guessed the wrong issue
- add another copy or skip a book you already own
- set the grade, slab company, cert number and what you paid
- send a book to your wantlist instead

Then one button adds them all. If you refresh or hit back by accident halfway through, the stack is still there when you come back.

Single scans got the same details panel, tucked behind "Add details" so the fast way stays fast.

The daily limit is still 100 scans, and books we don't have in the catalog don't count against it.

## Selling got a little more real

The marketplace is still a beta. There's still no checkout, buyers still message you, and you two sort out payment (PayPal Goods & Services, please). But:

- When you list a book, you get a quick prompt to add a price and a photo of your actual copy. The For sale tab in your library tells you how many of your listings still need a price, and you can price them in a row without opening each one.
- When a book sells, mark it sold and enter what it went for. It comes off your shelf and goes into a private Sold history that only you can see, with your totals.

Your sold history is private. Other members can't see what you sold something for.

## A fresh coat of paint

Account settings got rebuilt so they don't look like a tax form. Anyone without a profile photo now gets their own colorway of the CC badge (there are twelve, and yes, one of them is very Venom). And big collections load a lot faster on profiles. A 200-book profile used to take about five seconds to show up. Now it's usually under one.

## So what's Collector+?

Here's the deal: everything you need to catalog, grade, value and track your collection is free. Grading, slab and cert tracking, photos of your copies, value charts, the insurance PDF and full exports all used to be Pro features. As of last week they're free for everyone.

The first 100 members get Collector Pro free for life, no card. Most of those spots are still open, and you don't have to do anything special: [sign up](https://www.comixcatalog.com/signup) while spots are left and one is yours. The [founding collectors page](https://www.comixcatalog.com/founding-collectors) shows how many are left.

After those are gone, the way to support the site is [Collector+](https://www.comixcatalog.com/upgrade): $4 a month or $36 a year. You get a Collector+ badge on your profile and no upgrade prompts. That's it. I'm not going to pretend a badge is a reason to pay. The reason is that this is an independent site run by one guy who collects, and Collector+ is how you help keep the lights on.

If you've got a friend with a closet full of long boxes, now is a good time to send them the link. Then follow them.
