Right now there are 370 comics listed on the ComixCatalog marketplace, and exactly zero of them have a price on them. Twenty-four of those are mine. I am aware of the irony.

So before I start asking people to list their books, I want to be straight about what the marketplace is today, what it is not, and what I fixed this week so it is worth your time.

## What it is

Every book in your library can be listed for sale. You mark it for sale, set a price (or leave it open to offers), add photos of the actual copy and your condition notes, and it shows up on the [marketplace](/marketplace) and on that issue's page.

Buyers browse by publisher, series, grade, decade or price. When they find something, they message you. You two work out payment and shipping.

## What it is not

There is no checkout. No money moves through ComixCatalog, so there is no buyer protection from us, no payouts and no fees. "No fees during the beta" is not a promo. There is literally nothing to charge a fee on.

There are no seller ratings yet either. What you can see about a seller is their username, how long they have been collecting here, the rest of what they have for sale, and their public collection.

If checkout ever happens, anything you list now carries over. You will not have to list it again.

## What changed this week

**Prices are real now.** If a seller had not set a price, the marketplace used to show an estimate built from eBay asking prices. It looked like a price. It was not one. Now a book shows the seller's price or "Open to offers," and the estimate only shows up for the seller, as a hint while they decide what to ask.

**Report and block.** Every listing, profile and conversation has a Report link. Reports come straight to me. Every conversation has a Block button, and someone you block cannot message you.

**Limits on brand-new accounts.** An account under a week old can only send so many messages an hour and start a few new conversations a day. If you are a real person, you will never notice. If you are a spam bot, you will notice immediately.

**You find out when someone writes.** When someone messages you, you get an email with a link to the conversation. It never includes the message itself, and it is one email per conversation every few hours at most. You can turn it off in your [account settings](/account#notifications).

## How to trade without getting burned

This is the same advice I would give for any comic deal with a stranger:

- Pay with PayPal Goods & Services, not Friends & Family. Goods & Services is what gets you PayPal's buyer protection if a book never shows up or is not what was described.
- Ask for photos of the exact copy, front and back, before you pay.
- For slabbed books, look the cert number up on the grading company's site.
- Keep the conversation in ComixCatalog messages so there is a record.
- If someone pushes gift cards, wire transfers or a payment app with no protection, walk away.

All of that lives on the [Trust & Safety page](/trust) too.

## If you have a long box

List it. Even ten books with a price and a couple of photos make the marketplace better for everyone, and I want to hear what is annoying about listing them so I can fix it. You can message me on the site or email comixcatalog@gmail.com.
