I've got every Manhattan Projects and East of West trade on my shelf. Until this week, a lot of trades like those were hard or flat out impossible to find on ComixCatalog. For a site that's supposed to catalog your whole collection, that's a pretty big blind spot. The trade shelf is usually the best looking part of the room.

So that got fixed. Here's what's new.

## More than 17,000 trades you couldn't find before

Most trade paperbacks, hardcovers and omnibuses are listed as a single book with no issue number, and search used to skip those. Not anymore. All told, close to 27,000 collected editions show up in search now.

## They're tagged

In search, a trade carries a little tag like TPB, HC, Omnibus or Deluxe, so you can tell East of West the trade from East of West the issues at a glance.

![Search results for East of West, with the TPB and Compendium tags on the collected editions](https://wlrcxnzdywzxgrcqvctc.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/comic-covers/blog/trade-paperbacks/01-search.jpg)

## You add them like anything else

Open the book's page and hit Add to collection or Add to wantlist, same as a single issue. If it's a numbered run of trades, like the ten East of West TPBs, the page lists every volume and Mark what you own works on them too.

![The East of West TPB page, listing each volume with its cover](https://wlrcxnzdywzxgrcqvctc.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/comic-covers/blog/trade-paperbacks/02-trade-page.jpg)

## Finding your edition

Plenty of books got collected more than once: a paperback, a hardcover, a deluxe edition, a reprint from another publisher. Each one is its own entry, and the tag, the year and the publisher help you tell them apart.

One tip: if the trade has the same name as the series, search shows the series first. Add tpb, hc or omnibus to the search and you get just the collected editions, so "saga tpb" goes straight to the Saga trades. The search dropdown also has a Trades link at the bottom that does the same thing.

## Covers are catching up

Here's the honest part: most trades don't have a cover on here yet. About 3,700 have at least one, and about 2,500 trade covers came in since Monday. I've got the cover pipeline working through trades now, so more show up every day.

Brand new trades are the slowest, because a lot of this year's books don't have a cover we can get yet. A book without a cover still works. Add it to your collection and it shows a placeholder until the cover comes in.
