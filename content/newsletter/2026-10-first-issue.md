Hey. You signed up for this at some point, probably in a footer, and this is the first time I have actually sent one. Sorry about the gap. There was no way to send email until about a week ago, which is a funny thing to admit in an email.

Here is what this is going to be: one message every few weeks, from me, about what got added to the catalog, what got fixed, and a book or two worth going to find. No daily drip. One click to leave at the bottom, and it works the first time.

## What ComixCatalog is, in one paragraph

Every series, every issue, every printing, with the covers attached to the right book. You mark what you own and what you are hunting. The site keeps score: how much of a run you have, what it is worth based on real sales, and which issues are still out there. It is the list I always wanted to carry into the shop instead of a vague feeling that I "need some Daredevil."

## What changed this month

- **Search actually finds the run you meant.** Spider-Man has been restarted more times than my router. Results now group by volume with the years next to each one.
- **Thousands of covers moved to the right series.** Same-titled runs (Wolverine 1988 vs 2020, Fables the monthly vs Fables the trades) were getting each other's art. A lot of that is fixed, and the site now knows which "series" are actually trade paperbacks.
- **Key issues.** First appearances and the big deaths are flagged on the issue page, with a line on why they matter.
- **The Instagram account stopped being a robot.** Same guy who writes this now writes those. [@comixcatalog](https://www.instagram.com/comixcatalog), if you want covers with opinions.

## One book to go find

**The Batman Adventures #12 (1993).** First comic-book appearance of Harley Quinn. She was invented for the animated series and this is where she crossed over. If Batman: The Animated Series got to you as a kid, this is the one. Raw copies still turn up in bins for less than you would think. [Catalog page](https://www.comixcatalog.com/search?q=batman%20adventures)
