# ComixCatalog video format

## Opening

Open with a 5–10 second cold open that earns the click, hit the 2–3 second `ChannelSting`, then go straight into the premise. Do not open with “hey guys, welcome back” or a long channel introduction.

## Visual language

- Cyclops blue and gold are the core palette.
- Use halftone texture, narration boxes, real comic covers, and deliberate motion.
- Avoid generic generated imagery and anything with an AI-art look.
- Put Tony on camera for the cold open, transitions, and ending only. Let the books and supporting visuals carry the body.

## Shorts

Every Short is a standalone script with its own hook, enough context to make sense without the long video, and its own ending. Record Shorts separately; do not define them as ranges cut from the episode narration. Episode configs and `audio/shorts/` reserve their scripts and recordings until standalone rendering lands.
