# ComixCatalog Episode 001 — Visual Edit Blueprint
## Where to Start Reading X-Men Without Losing Your Mind

**Target runtime:** 16:11  
**Editing approach:** deterministic visual edit; no Underlord art direction  
**Primary visual sources:** uploaded comic covers, official Marvel/Disney promotional imagery, actual ComixCatalog UI, restrained stock/context footage

---

## Global edit rules

- Change the image roughly every **4–9 seconds** during dense sections and every **8–14 seconds** during reflective sections.
- Do not leave a generic title card on screen when a relevant real cover exists.
- Prefer **real comic covers, real X-Men promotional imagery, real ComixCatalog captures** over generated approximations.
- Use cover art in several ways: full cover, cropped detail, two-cover split, three-cover stack, slow push, pan across typography, issue-number/title overlay.
- Keep title cards short: usually 2–4 seconds.
- Avoid meme-style GIF spam. Animated material should support a specific narration beat.
- The Dark Phoenix section should feel like the visual centerpiece.
- Keep the ComixCatalog section clean and brief; it should feel like the natural tool behind the recommendation, not an ad break.
