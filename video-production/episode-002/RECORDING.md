# Episode 002 recording checklist

## Setup

- Keep the mic close.
- Record narration at 48 kHz WAV in a quiet room.
- Record 10 seconds of room tone.
- Record paragraph by paragraph. Pause 2 seconds between paragraphs. Redo a flubbed paragraph immediately.

## B-roll

Framing rule: Film overhead or chest-down. Tony's face must never be in frame. A phone on a stand or overhead mount is fine. Use soft, even light. Avoid backlight.

| What | Duration | Framing or angle | Props or issues | Speaks or silent B-roll | Screen substitute |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| Shelf and long box | 20 s each | Wide, then close slow push | Tony's real collection | Silent | No |
| Pull MP and EoW copies | 20 s | Overhead hands | Confirmed owned issues | Silent | No |
| Flip through long box | 30 s | Overhead, titles readable | Real books only | Silent | No |
| Optional GPK card | 8 s | Close-up | Tony's owned card only | Silent | No |

## Pre-record questions

- Which Manhattan Projects issue will Tony hold in P28?
- Which Manhattan Projects and East of West issues does Tony own?
- Did Tony buy them monthly as they came out, and roughly when and where?
- Does Tony remember seeing the East of West covers on the rack? If yes, what does he remember?
- Should P20, the MCU paragraph, stay?
- Is P58 true? Is Tony going back through the books?
- Has Death's white outfit been checked against the available art?
- Are the duplicate ComixCatalog series rows fixed before any optional screen recording?
