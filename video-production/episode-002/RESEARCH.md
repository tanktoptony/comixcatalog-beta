## Things deliberately left out
- **Exact sales numbers** (Wikipedia cites 1.5M for East of West). Weak sourcing, not needed.
- **The Oppenheimer twist, Fermi's secret, Death's family.** All early-issue reveals. The script teases them and doesn't tell.
- **Ultimate Spider-Man, G.O.D.S., Imperial.** Real, recent Marvel work, but the brief says keep Marvel brief.
- **Three Worlds, Three Moons (Substack, 2021).** Real, but a distribution detour that needs its own explanation.
- **The Illuminati's origin.** Hickman didn't create it (Bendis did, 2006). The script only credits him with incursions.
