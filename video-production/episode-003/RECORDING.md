# Episode 003 recording checklist

## Setup

- Keep the mic close.
- Record narration at 48 kHz WAV in a quiet room.
- Record 10 seconds of room tone.
- Record paragraph by paragraph. Pause 2 seconds between paragraphs. Redo a flubbed paragraph immediately.

## Pre-record questions

- Who is Corey, how does Tony know him, and why did Corey give him the boxes?
- Does Corey consent to use of his name, voice, or any footage of him?
- What Coheed book or run was Tony trying to find?
- What did the room look like after sorting?
- Which mainstream series were in the boxes?
- Which Malibu titles were in the boxes?
- Did the QA punch-list books come from Corey's boxes?
- Is there still a book Tony cannot identify?
- Did Tony personally hit the Cerebus and Malibu search gaps with these boxes?
- What real UX frustration happened during cataloging?
- Should Slave Labor and Fireman Press be added to the allowlist before recording?
- What do the Milk & Cheese, Scud, Cerebus, Malibu, and marketplace checks show on recording day?
- Is Episode 004 locked as Coheed and Cambria?
