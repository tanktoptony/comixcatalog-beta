## The method the script uses

Only one song-to-story link is sourced as an official statement (Jersey City Devils, RS). Every other link in the script comes from **what the lyric itself says**: a character's name, a ship's crew, a letter's addressee. The script says "the lyrics name X" or "this song sounds like it takes place on a ship", never "Claudio says this song is about X". That's how the handoff's draft-2 `[VERIFY]` notes on tracks 2 and 4 to 10 are resolved.

## The 4 open [VERIFY] lines (keep or cut while recording)

1. **The Hearshot Kid** (section 09). Only WIKI-C says a prisoner on Shylos-10 called "the Hearshot Kid" appears in chapter 7 (Amory Wars II #2), told by a character named Cecil. CV has no summary for the 2008 issues. If you have the Ultimate Edition or the 2008 issues, it's in chapter 7. If you'd rather not risk it, cut the last two paragraphs of section 09 and the "one of these song titles" line in section 02.
2. **Junesong is Newo's nickname** (section 11). Only the fan wiki's character infobox lists "Junesong" as Newo's alias. Cut the one sentence if unsure. The "Dear Newo Ikkin" lyric is solid either way.
3. **Patrick Ewing** (section 10). Fan-wiki trivia. Ewing did wear 33 for the Knicks; the naming link is a fan theory, and the script says so. Keep or cut.
4. **Ages**. The script avoids exact ages (WIKI-C has Josephine "almost 23", Claudio "around 17") on purpose.

## Spoiler and content lines

- Stops at the end of chapter 5 (the ship goodbye). The 2008 issues appear only as covers, plus the Hearshot Kid mention and the "last line" note, neither of which reveals what happens to Coheed and Cambria.
- No "Crowing". Claudio's powers are shown (revival, invisibility) and left unexplained.
- Josephine's assault: one sentence in section 06. No depiction. The twins and Josephine's death are told in single restrained lines, with album art on screen and no panels.
