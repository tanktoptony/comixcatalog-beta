Recording script, draft 3 (2026-10-19). Built from Tony's draft 2 handoff, the Part 2 research sheet, the album's lyric sheet, and the comic chapter summaries in `RESEARCH.md`.

How to read this file:
- Each `##` heading is one recording. Save it under that file name.
- Narration is plain text. Read it as written, or loosen it. Short pauses between paragraphs give me cut points.
- `MUSIC:` lines are for the edit. The real SSTB track opens each track chapter for 8 to 12 seconds, then drops under your voice.
- `SHOW:` lines are for the edit. Covers, album art, lyric cards and archive captures are already on hand. `[SCAN]` is optional: a phone photo of a page from your own copy makes that shot better, but the cut works without it.
- `[VERIFY]` marks the 4 lines I couldn't confirm from a second source. Keep or cut them as you read (details in RESEARCH.md).
- Spoiler line: nothing about how things end for Coheed and Cambria, and nobody says "the Crowing." The assault is one sentence and never shown.

Length: about 3,000 words of narration plus about 2.5 minutes of music, so roughly 22 to 24 minutes.

## 03-the-record.wav

Quick setup, since we covered the backstory last time.

The Second Stage Turbine Blade came out March 5, 2002, on Equal Vision Records. They recorded it in the fall of 2001 at a studio in Paramus, New Jersey.

Claudio Sanchez on vocals and rhythm guitar. Travis Stever on lead guitar. Michael Todd on bass. Josh Eppard on drums and piano. And a drummer named Nate Kelley plays on two of these songs. Remember that, because both of them are favorites of mine.

> SHOW: Album art. Lineup card over Wikimedia photos (Coheed live 2007, Travis Stever 2014). Credits on screen.

I heard this album on a burned CD in my dorm in the fall of 2002. I was there for the music. The dark stuff. The proggy guitars. The solos. I've always loved a good guitar solo.

The story came later. That's what the comics are for.

> SHOW: Wayback capture of coheedandcambria.com, October 2002, SSTB page.

This story got turned into comics more than once. The one we're following today is The Amory Wars: The Second Stage Turbine Blade. Ten chapters, 2007 and 2008. That's the complete version. We'll get to the others at the end.

> SHOW: Amory Wars (2007) #1 to #5, then Amory Wars II (2008) #1 to #5, as a grid.

Last thing. The songs and the comics don't line up one to one. The album came first, and the songs jump around between characters and moments. So when I say a song lines up with a scene, I'm going by what the lyrics actually say.

## 14-three-versions.wav · The comics

Quick word on the comics themselves, because this story got printed three different ways.

The first try was The Bag On Line Adventures in 2004. Two issues, art by Wes Abbott, and then it stopped. I bought both at a Hot Topic in Indianapolis when I was at Purdue. I wanted anything Coheed I could get my hands on.

> SHOW: Bag On Line #1 and #2 pair. [SCAN optional: one Wes Abbott page from your copy.]

Then in 2007 it started over as The Amory Wars. Evil Ink with Image Comics and 12-Gauge. Ten chapters across 2007 and 2008. Gus Vazquez drew the first two, Mike S. Miller the next three, and Gabriel Guzman the last five. Tony Moore, from The Walking Dead, did the covers. And there was a variant cover sold only at Hot Topic. Of course there was.

> SHOW: 2007 #1 to #5 then 2008 #1 to #5 with artist labels.

The 2007 art was kind of meh to me. But I wanted the rest of the story, and the art wasn't going to stop me.

> SHOW: [SCAN optional: two pages from the 2007 run you'd pick.]

In 2010, BOOM! Studios collected all of it in a hardcover Ultimate Edition. About 350 pages. All ten chapters, the two original Wes Abbott issues, and the covers.

> SHOW: Ultimate Edition cover. [SCAN optional: your copy on a table.]

All of these are on ComixCatalog. The Bag On Line issues, the 2007 and 2008 series, and the Ultimate Edition. You can look up every issue, add the ones you own to your collection, and put the ones you're missing on your wantlist. Signing up is free. Link's in the description.
