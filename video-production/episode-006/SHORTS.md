## short-1-not-iron-man.wav · "Doom isn't just Iron Man with a cape"

Doctor Doom is not Iron Man with a cape. Here's the proof.

In 1989, Marvel put out Triumph and Torment. Doctor Strange and Doctor Doom. Roger Stern and Mike Mignola.

It opens with a contest. Marvel's sorcerers, competing for the title of Sorcerer Supreme. And Doom gets one rule. Turn off the tech in your gauntlets. Magic only.

He does it. And it comes down to two guys. Strange and Doom.

Strange keeps the title. But in this contest the winner owes the runner-up a favor. Strange is worried Doom's going to ask for help taking over the world. Doom says he doesn't need help with that.

What he wants is his mother's soul back from Mephisto.

So yeah, he builds the armor. He also nearly beat the Sorcerer Supreme with the armor switched off.

Full Doom dossier is on the channel.

## short-2-silver-surfer.wav · "The time Doom stole the Silver Surfer's powers"

Doctor Doom once stole the Silver Surfer's powers by giving him a tour of his castle.

Fantastic Four 57. 1966. Doom invites the Surfer over to Latveria and shows him around. Then, in the lab, he distracts him with a view of the cosmos the Surfer misses so much, hooks him up, and drains the Power Cosmic. Takes the board too.

Next issue, he flattens the Fantastic Four so badly Reed tells his own team to stand down. And Doom lets them live. Because losing is worse.

So how do you beat that? Reed's answer: make him angry.

In issue 60, Reed sends a little flying wing to smack Doom and fly off. Doom chases it. Straight up. Right into the barrier Galactus put around Earth to keep the Surfer from leaving.

Flash. Power gone.

Reed didn't out-punch him. He out-annoyed him. Fantastic Four 57 to 60.

## short-3-god-emperor.wav · "Why God Emperor Doom doesn't count"

When people say Doctor Doom beat the Beyonder, they're right. It still doesn't count.

Here's Marvel's own power grid for Doom. Out of seven. Intelligence six. Strength four. Speed five. Durability six. Energy six. Fighting four. That's Doom in his armor.

Now, 1984. The original Secret Wars. Doom builds a machine out of a villain named Klaw. Literally out of him. Uses it to drain Galactus. Then takes the Beyonder's power. He's a god for about two issues, until his own doubts let the Beyonder take it back.

2015. Jonathan Hickman's Secret Wars. Doom takes the power of the Beyonders and builds what's left of reality into Battleworld. God Emperor Doom. And by the end, he admits Reed would have done better with it. [VERIFY wording]

Borrowed power. Temporary, every time. So it goes on the card as an overlay. Not the baseline.

Standard Doom is scary enough. Full dossier on the channel.
