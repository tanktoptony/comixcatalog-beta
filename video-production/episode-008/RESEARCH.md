## Facts: The Nice House on the Lake

Creative team (source: ComicVine issue credits; cover trade dress)
- Writer James Tynion IV, artist Álvaro Martínez Bueno, colorist Jordie Bellaire, letters by AndWorld Design. Editor Chris Conroy. Main covers by Martínez Bueno.
- Published by DC under its Black Label imprint (mature readers). Every Lake cover carries the Black Label logo.
- Martínez Bueno and Tynion had worked together before on DC superhero books (GamesRadar headline: "Batman's Tynion & Martinez reunite for an original horror comic at DC"). [VERIFY which books: Detective Comics and Justice League Dark are likely.]

Issues and dates (source: ComicVine on-sale dates)
- 12 issues, titled "Book One" through "Book Twelve".
- #1 on sale June 1, 2021. #6 November 16, 2021. #7 March 1, 2022. #9 May 31, 2022. #10 September 27, 2022. #12 December 27, 2022.
- It took two breaks: about three and a half months between #6 and #7, and about four months between #9 and #10. No published reason found. [VERIFY before saying why.]
- Collected: Vol. 1 (#1 to 6) March 1, 2022; Vol. 2 (#7 to 12) March 7, 2023; The Deluxe Edition hardcover (#1 to 12) October 24, 2023 (source: Wikipedia "The Nice House", ComicVine).
- Free Comic Book Day 2026 (May 2, 2026): DC gave away The Nice House on the Lake #1 FCBD 2026 Special Edition, a 48-page sampler (source: DC's FCBD 2026 announcement, retailer listings). Its cover carries the Vertigo logo.
- #1 went back to press: a second printing came out in July 2021 with a distressed version of the cover and a 1:25 variant (source: Bleeding Cool PrintWatch, Key Collector). A third printing is listed by price guides. [VERIFY third printing before saying it.] No sales numbers found. Don't claim sales figures.
- #1 had 21 variant and retailer-exclusive covers listed on ComicVine (artists include Francesco Francavilla, Martin Simmonds, Robert Hack, John Gallagher, Werther Dell'Edera, Ken Lashley, Megan Hutchison, David Romero, John Giang). Each later issue had one variant.

The cover trick (source: the covers themselves)
- Every main cover has a row of ten small symbols under the logo, one per guest. Each issue lights up one symbol (#3 lights the third, #7 the seventh). Each issue is built around one guest. [VERIFY the exact symbol-to-character mapping before naming characters on screen.] Book Six's row adds an extra symbol.

Premise (sources: Hollywood Reporter announcement; DC; Wikipedia)
- Walter, "weird, sure, but seemingly harmless," invites a group of friends, mostly 30-somethings, to a week at a beautiful lake house. The house was first believed to be in Wisconsin (Wikipedia). Fun fact for a Chicagoland host: Tynion grew up in Milwaukee, Wisconsin (Wikipedia, "James Tynion IV"). [VERIFY the Wisconsin detail in the book itself before saying it on air.]
- The world outside ends. They can have anything they want in the house, but they can never leave.
- Ten guests, each with a role: Ryan Cane (the Artist), Norah Jakobs (the Writer), David Daye (the Comedian), Molly Reynolds (the Accountant), Veronica Wright (the Scientist), Sam Nguyen (the Reporter), Arturo Pérez (the Acupuncturist), Sarah Radnitz (the Consultant), Naya Radia (the Doctor), Rick MacEwan (the Pianist). An eleventh, Reg Madison, turns up later. (Source: Epic Panels issue guide, Wikipedia. [VERIFY spellings against the book.])
- Tynion said the core idea came about five years before the 2020 announcement and coalesced over the following years (Hollywood Reporter). The pandemic made its themes of isolation more relevant: that's the reporter's framing, not Tynion's. Don't say the pandemic inspired it.
- Tynion at C2E2 2026 (Popverse, June 11, 2026): "The Nice House on the Lake is a very personal horror story for me," "the villain of that story is very much based on me. The character of Walter," and Walter "looks a lot like me. That's on purpose." Also: "Writing is always a kind of therapy."
- Tynion calls his earlier BOOM! series The Woods "the blueprint of everything I've done since" and says Nice House "taps into a lot of the same core fears" (Bleeding Cool, June 2024).

Awards (sources in brackets)
- 2022 Eisner Award, Best New Series: The Nice House on the Lake. Tynion also won Best Writer that year (ComicBook.com, Wikipedia). The Vol. 2 trade cover says "Winner of two 2022 Will Eisner Awards: Best New Series and Best Writer."
- 2023 Eisner Awards: Tynion, Best Writer (for The Nice House on the Lake and The Sandman Universe: Nightmare Country); Jordie Bellaire, Best Coloring (for The Nice House on the Lake and Suicide Squad: Blaze). Nice House was nominated for Best Continuing Series; Nightwing won (AIPT, DC.com, Comics Beat 2023 coverage).
- 2024 Angoulême International Comics Festival (France): the series prize (Fauve Prix de la Série) went to the French edition of The Nice House on the Lake Vol. 2, published by Urban Comics (Livres Hebdo, Mollat, Comics Beat).
- Nominations per Wikipedia: 2022 Harvey Award Book of the Year; 2023 GLAAD Media Award, Outstanding Comic Book. [VERIFY before saying.]
- Safe one-liner: "It won the Eisner for Best New Series, and two more Eisners the next year, and a top prize at Angoulême in France."

## Facts: The Nice House by the Sea

- Announced April 16, 2024 as a 13-issue series (CBR); it shipped as 12 issues.
- Same team: Tynion, Martínez Bueno, Bellaire, AndWorld Design. Launch variants by Nick Robles, Hayden Sherman and a 1:25 by Dani (CBR).
- #1 on sale July 24, 2024. #6 March 5, 2025. #7 February 4, 2026. #12 (final) October 14, 2026 (ComicVine on-sale dates). An 11-month gap between #6 and #7. No published reason found.
- Imprint: #1 to 3 carry the DC Black Label logo; #4 (October 30, 2024) onward carry the revived Vertigo logo (the covers themselves). DC relaunched Vertigo and moved some Black Label titles into it (Wikipedia).
- Collected: Vol. 1 (#1 to 6) March 4, 2025 (Wikipedia). [VERIFY: Vol. 2 date not announced in our sources.]
- Premise (CBR): a different house, by the sea, with a different host, Max, who invites strangers, promising they will survive and shape humanity's future. Their roles: a Doctor, Writer, Historian, Actor, Artist, Priest, Scientist, Singer, Politician and Mathematician.
- Tynion on the tone (CBR): "This is a house of strangers and tension much more than a house of friendship." The characters "do not like each other."
- 2025 GLAAD Media Award nominee, Outstanding Comic Book (Wikipedia). [VERIFY]

## How the two series connect (spoilers, see map)

- Lake ends with "End of Cycle One." Its last issue reveals other survivor groups in separate "ecosystems" (CBR, January 2023).
- Sea issues tease the overseer of the Sea house checking on the Lake house (#5), an invasion of the Lake house (#8), and Walter and Max at war with both houses collapsing in #12 (Comic Watch and Graphic Policy solicitation text). AIPT's #12 review says the finale puts Norah in control as the two worlds head toward destruction and leaves room for hope.
- No third series has been announced as of November 17, 2026. [VERIFY right before publishing.]
