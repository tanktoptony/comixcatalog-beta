## The album

Sources: Wikipedia "In Keeping Secrets of Silent Earth: 3"; Coheed fan wiki (coheed.fandom.com) where marked.
- Coheed and Cambria's second album. Released October 7, 2003 on Equal Vision Records (Columbia is also listed).
- Recorded at Applehead Recording in Woodstock, New York. Produced by Michael Birnbaum and Chris Bittner.
- Lineup: Claudio Sanchez (vocals, guitar), Travis Stever (guitar), Michael Todd (bass), Josh Eppard (drums). Danny Louis, additional keyboards.
- Peaked at No. 52 on the Billboard 200. Certified Gold by the RIAA (500,000 units). Entertainment Weekly called it one of the top five key albums of the new prog genre. [VERIFY the EW wording before quoting it.]
- Singles: "A Favor House Atlantic" (June 8, 2004), "Blood Red Summer" (November 9, 2004), the title track (2005).
- Music videos (fan wiki): "A Favor House Atlantic" was directed by Christian Winters, the band in an '80s rock bar, played for laughs. "Blood Red Summer" has Claudio alone in a cabin in the woods setting traps. [VERIFY before describing on air; we can't show the videos.]
- Tracklist (Wikipedia, fan wiki):
  1. The Ring in Return (2:07)
  2. In Keeping Secrets of Silent Earth: 3 (8:15)
  3. Cuts Marked in the March of Men (5:05)
  4. Three Evils (Embodied in Love and Shadow) (5:10)
  5. The Crowing (6:40)
  6. Blood Red Summer (4:05)
  7. The Camper Velourium I: Faint of Hearts (5:21)
  8. The Camper Velourium II: Backend of Forever (5:23)
  9. The Camper Velourium III: Al the Killer (4:15)
  10. A Favor House Atlantic (3:55)
  11. The Light & the Glass (9:40)
  Then tracks 12 to 22 are silent filler ("A Lot of Nothing I to XI"), and track 23 is the hidden song "21:13" (9:50). Digital versions and reissues drop the silence and 21:13; the vinyl includes 21:13 (Wikipedia).
- "21:13" reprises "Time Consumer" and "IRO-Bot" from the first album, and is the band's longest song (fan wiki). The lyric "And the clock reads 21:13" gives it the name.
- "The Ring in Return" opens with a spoken line: "Hello Apollo, where should I begin?" (fan wiki lyrics). The comic's issue #1 opens the same way: Claudio telling the story to Apollo outside Newo Ikkin's house. That's the cleanest album-to-comic link in the whole chapter.
- Song titles that are story names (stated as titles, not as Claudio's word): "The Crowing" (Claudio's role in the story), the three "Camper Velourium" songs (the Camper Velourium is a ship in the story), "Al the Killer" (a freighter pilot in the story).
- Vinyl: a remastered 2LP was announced in August 2014 (grey marbled, a peach marbled sold only on the Neverender tour, black 180g) (Wikipedia). An anniversary-edition vinyl reissue is listed by retailers on Sony Legacy. [VERIFY the year before saying it.]

## The story (album and comic)

Sources: Wikipedia; Coheed fan wiki (Evil Ink's own summary, plus its issue #1 summary).
- Ten years after The Second Stage Turbine Blade. Claudio Kilgannon comes up out of Shylos Ten, the "quiet" planet where the Red Army runs its interrogations and prisons. He finds out his entire family has been murdered and starts out on a "quest for vendetta" (Evil Ink summary).
- Wilhelm Ryan (the Supreme Tri-Mage) and General Mayo Deftinwolf sense he's alive and has special powers, and they need to stop him.
- Inferno (Jesse) is waging his own war on the Red Army from his ship, the Grail Arbor.
- Claudio teams up with Ambellina, a Prise cast out by her own kind and forced to be his guide, and Sizer, a disassembled IRO-bot. They go looking for Inferno for answers.
- Their plans go sideways on a ship called the Camper Velourium, with a freighter pilot called Al the Killer.
- Issue #1 details (fan wiki): Claudio is haunted by his father's ghost, which only he can see and hear, and they argue. Inferno's lab is building two IRO-bots, Chase and Sizer; Chase is kept with the mind of a grown woman in the body of a little girl. Claudio uses his powers for the first time in ten years, and a little girl calls him an angel. He tells her there are no angels anymore.
