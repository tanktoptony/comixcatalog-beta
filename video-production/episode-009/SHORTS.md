# Episode 009 Shorts (Tony's words, 2026-11-21)

Three standalone Shorts. `>` lines are visual and clip notes, not narration.

## short-1 · The album with eleven silent tracks (~40 s)

Imagine buying a CD, getting to the end of the album, and finding eleven tracks of absolutely nothing. That's what Coheed and Cambria did with In Keeping Secrets of Silent Earth: 3 in two thousand three.

> SHOW: Album cover. [SCAN: Physical CD and track listing.]

After "The Light & the Glass," tracks twelve through twenty-two are silent. But if you keep going, track twenty-three has a surprise. A hidden, nearly ten-minute song called "21:13."

> SHOW: IKSSE:3 and SSTB album covers.

It even reprises parts of "Time Consumer" and "I-Robot" from their first album. Some digital versions removed the hidden track entirely.

> SHOW: Both album covers side by side.

I loved finding stuff like this. It felt like a secret. And the title? It comes from the lyric, "And the clock reads 21:13."

> SHOW: IKSSE:3 album cover, slow push inward. CLIP: "21:13", maximum 5 seconds before the final line.

## short-2 · Hello Apollo (~40 s)

"Hello Apollo, where should I begin?"

That's the first spoken line on Coheed and Cambria's second album, In Keeping Secrets of Silent Earth: 3. It's also how the comic starts.

> SHOW: Album cover beside issue #1 cover. CLIP: "The Ring in Return", opening line, maximum 5 seconds.

Open the comic and it's the same scene. Claudio is talking to Apollo outside Newo Ikkin's house.

> SHOW: Issue #1 cover, Apollo and Newo character art. [SCAN: Opening page of issue #1.]

I first heard this album in college in two thousand three. More than twenty years later, I'm still finding story in it.

> SHOW: Album cover transitioning into issue #1.

## short-3 · Comic-Con exclusives for a rock band's comic (~40 s)

You know what's dangerous about being both a comic collector and a Coheed and Cambria fan? Discovering that their comic books had convention-exclusive covers.

> SHOW: Issue #1 standard cover followed by convention-exclusive cover.

When BOOM! Studios published The Amory Wars: In Keeping Secrets of Silent Earth: 3, there were twelve issues. Issue one had a convention-exclusive cover limited to five hundred copies.

> SHOW: Actual #1 convention-exclusive cover.

Then issue nine got a C2E2 exclusive limited to just three hundred copies. C2E2 is Chicago's comic con, so that one gets me.

> SHOW: Actual #9 C2E2 exclusive cover.

I bought all twelve original issues as they came out, but I don't have either of those exclusives. Believe me, I wish I did.

> SHOW: Standard #1–12 cover montage, then the exclusive covers side by side.

So here's a question for the collectors. Do you own one of these? Because I'd really like to see it.

> SHOW: #1 convention and #9 C2E2 covers side by side.
